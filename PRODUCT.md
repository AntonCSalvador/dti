# Product

## Platform

Local browser companion for the Roblox desktop player.

## Users and task

A Dress To Impress player enters the round theme, compares achievable reference outfits, and follows a free-item dressing recipe while playing. Both feminine and masculine styles and both avatar bodies are supported.

## Confirmed constraints

- V1 supplies manual instructions. Desktop game control is a v2 release.
- Only permanent standard/free dressing-room items.
- Live visual references plus a prepared catalog; recoloring and toggles are central to matching.
- Recommendations must be grounded in catalog entries and show uncertain or unreproducible details.

## Implementation defaults

Next.js/React/TypeScript, local hosting, OpenAI vision, and SerpApi Google Images were selected as practical build defaults. User-specific framework/hosting preferences remain open; no public deployment is configured.

## Evidence

`data/catalog.json` contains source-documented items and provenance. No real-round win-rate data, paid-provider quality evaluation, or in-game assembly validation exists yet.
